# Enorsis Procurement Planning and Savings

## Annual procurement plans

Plans establish:

- Fiscal year
- Strategic objective
- Approved procurement budget
- Savings target
- Committed and actual spend
- Realized savings
- Approval ownership

## Category strategies

Strategies connect market intelligence, supplier posture, sourcing, contracts,
risk, addressable spend and savings targets for a procurement category.

## Savings initiatives

Supported initiative types:

```text
COST_REDUCTION
COST_AVOIDANCE
WORKING_CAPITAL
DEMAND_REDUCTION
PROCESS_EFFICIENCY
RISK_AVOIDANCE
```

Lifecycle:

```text
IDEA
VALIDATED
APPROVED
IN_EXECUTION
REALIZED
CANCELLED
```

Finance validation and realized-value fields remain distinct to preserve
governance between procurement claims and financial recognition.

## Savings milestones

Milestones track execution dates, ownership, blockers and evidence.

## Current release

This release establishes governed planning and savings records. Automated links
to sourcing awards, contracts, invoices and spend baselines can be added through
the existing source type and source ID fields.
