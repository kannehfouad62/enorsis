# Enorsis Asset Lifecycle and Equipment Management

This module adds procured-asset registration, custody assignments, location,
capital value, useful life, warranties, preventive and corrective maintenance,
calibration, service history, downtime, evidence, retirement and disposal.

Run:

```bash
python3 scripts/update-enterprise-modules-for-assets.py
```

to add the workspace to the Procurement section of `/app/modules`.
