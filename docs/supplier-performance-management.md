# Enorsis Supplier Performance Management

## Scorecard model

Scorecards combine eight weighted categories:

```text
Delivery       20%
Quality        20%
Cost           15%
Service        10%
Innovation     10%
ESG            10%
Risk           10%
Compliance      5%
```

Ratings are calculated as:

```text
90–100  EXCEPTIONAL
80–89   STRONG
70–79   ACCEPTABLE
55–69   NEEDS_IMPROVEMENT
0–54    CRITICAL
```

Scorecards progress from `DRAFT` to `IN_REVIEW` and then `PUBLISHED`.

## Evidence integration

The detail workspace displays the supplier's recent:

- Risk assessments
- Risk findings
- ESG assessments
- Purchase-order history

The first release records KPI values manually while preserving data-source and
evidence fields for automated calculations in a later release.

## Supplier development plans

Plans record objectives, owners, dates, success measures, actions, cadence and
progress.

## Supplier corrective actions

SCAR records include:

- Unique SCAR number
- Severity
- Source record
- Immediate containment
- Root cause
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Supplier contact
- Due date
- Verification and closure evidence
