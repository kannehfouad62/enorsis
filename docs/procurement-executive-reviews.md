# Enorsis Procurement Executive Reviews

Review types include weekly operating reviews, monthly and quarterly business
reviews, executive committee reviews, and board packs.

Each review stores a frozen reporting period, executive narrative, KPI
snapshots, decisions, risks, priorities, actions, approval, and publication
timestamps.

Run:

```bash
python3 scripts/update-enterprise-modules-for-reviews.py
```

to add Executive Reviews to the Intelligence section of `/app/modules`.
