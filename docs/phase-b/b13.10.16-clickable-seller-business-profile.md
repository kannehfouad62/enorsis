# B13.10.16 — Clickable Seller Business Profile

The previous Marketplace Seller Profile card was a static logo uploader and did
not navigate anywhere.

B13.10.16 creates a dedicated seller workspace:

`/app/marketplace/seller-profile`

The Marketplace Catalog profile card now contains an explicit:

`Edit seller profile →`

action.

Supplier and Buyer+Supplier administrators can maintain marketplace-facing:

- trading / marketplace business name
- website
- business email
- business phone
- business categories
- company logo

Legal company name, tenant country and governed operating-site location remain
synchronized from tenant organization data.

The self-supplier synchronization service is changed so it no longer overwrites
a seller-managed trading name each time the marketplace loads.

Seller profile updates are tenant-self scoped and audit logged as:

`marketplace.supplier.profile.update`

No Prisma migration is required beyond the B13.10.15 supplier-logo migration.
