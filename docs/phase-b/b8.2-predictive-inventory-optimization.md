# B8.2 — Predictive Inventory Optimization & Reorder Intelligence

Extends existing Enorsis Inventory, Replenishment and Demand Planning.

Capabilities:
- tenant-wide inventory aggregation
- forecast-horizon demand
- predicted reorder point
- recommended safety stock
- stockout probability
- estimated days of supply
- suggested reorder quantity
- excess quantity and estimated excess value
- lead-time-aware recommendations
- confidence and evidence per item
- persisted optimization runs and audit logging

Governance:
- no automatic InventoryItem policy changes
- no automatic replenishment/order creation
- operational execution remains in existing replenishment workflows
