# B13.10.15 — Supplier Business Logo Marketplace Profile

Supplier tenants can upload one company-level business logo for their Enorsis
marketplace seller identity.

The logo is not stored per product. It belongs to the tenant's self-supplier
profile and therefore appears consistently across all marketplace offerings.

## Seller experience

On `/app/marketplace/catalog`, Supplier and Buyer+Supplier tenants see a
Marketplace Seller Profile card.

They can:

- upload a JPG, PNG or WebP business logo
- replace the current logo
- preview the current logo
- use files up to 4 MB

The upload is stored in private Vercel Blob.

Only TENANT_OWNER, TENANT_ADMIN, SUPPLIER_MANAGER and Platform Super Admin
authorization paths may manage the tenant-self supplier logo.

## Buyer experience

The vendor-first Marketplace Catalog displays the business logo on each vendor
card.

After selecting a vendor, the vendor heading also displays the logo.

If no logo is configured, Enorsis falls back to the existing Building icon.

## Governance

Logo upload/replacement is audit logged as:

- `marketplace.supplier.logo.upload`
- `marketplace.supplier.logo.replace`

The registration endpoint verifies:

- authenticated tenant
- authorized role
- supplier belongs to the authenticated tenant
- supplier is the tenant self-profile
- blob path is under the supplier-specific marketplace prefix
