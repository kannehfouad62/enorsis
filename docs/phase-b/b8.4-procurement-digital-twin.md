# B8.4 — Procurement Digital Twin & Scenario Simulation

Capstone of Phase B8 Predictive Procurement.

Scenario inputs:
- demand shock %
- lead-time shock %
- cost inflation %
- supplier disruption %
- inbound reduction %
- safety-stock change %
- planning horizon

Simulation baselines:
- latest B8.1 predictive procurement forecast
- latest B8.2 predictive inventory optimization
- latest B8.3 predictive capacity plan
- marketplace supplier risk

Simulation outputs:
- spend impact
- demand impact
- inbound impact
- stockout-risk impact
- capacity-utilization impact
- supplier-risk impact
- severity by impact
- overall scenario risk
- governed recommendation

Governance:
- simulations are persisted
- baseline and scenario snapshots are persisted
- evidence is persisted per impact
- audit events are written for scenario creation and execution
- no live procurement, inventory, supplier, contract or warehouse data is mutated
