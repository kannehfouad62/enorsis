# B9.2 — Autonomous Strategy, Savings & Risk-Mitigation Recommendations

B9.2 converts approved B9.1 plans and predictive evidence into a governed
recommendation portfolio.

Recommendation classes:
- strategy recommendations
- savings opportunities
- risk-mitigation recommendations

Evidence sources:
- approved B9.1 autonomous procurement plan and plan actions
- B8.1 predictive procurement forecast
- B8.2 predictive inventory optimization
- B8.3 predictive capacity planning
- B8.4 digital-twin impacts

Savings governance:
- savings are planning hypotheses only
- excess-inventory opportunities use a conservative 15% value-recovery hypothesis
- planned sourcing actions use a conservative 3% sourcing-improvement hypothesis
- enterprise forecast spend uses a 2% optimization hypothesis
- all estimates require human validation before being called realized savings

Human governance:
- recommendation sets begin PENDING_REVIEW
- each recommendation must be ACCEPTED, REJECTED or DEFERRED by an authorized human
- all decisions are persisted and audited
- no disposition triggers execution

Governed AI:
- existing PROCUREMENT_COPILOT reviews deterministic recommendations
- AI cannot invent savings, suppliers, approvals, contracts, or transactions
- deterministic recommendations persist even if AI is unavailable
