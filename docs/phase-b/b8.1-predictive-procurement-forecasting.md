# B8.1 — Predictive Procurement Forecasting

Adds explainable predictive procurement forecasting without replacing the
existing Demand Planning or Spend Intelligence modules.

Forecast domains:
- spend forecasting from PurchaseRequest.usdEquivalent monthly history
- demand forecasting from DemandPlan forecast vs historical consumption
- supplier-risk forecasting from supplier master risk plus marketplace risk
  and performance evidence

Governance:
- deterministic and evidence-backed
- persisted forecast runs
- persisted forecast signals
- confidence and risk level
- source assumptions recorded with each run
- audit event for every forecast generation
- no autonomous operational action
